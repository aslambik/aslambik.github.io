From writing stories, to writing workflows.

Dari menulis cerita, ke menulis alur kerja.

I work at the intersection of automation and AI — designing n8n workflows that replace repetitive manual processes: from incoming forms, to document processing, to AI-assisted decisions, without anyone having to handle each step by hand.

My flagship project is an automated CV screening system — from an applicant filling out a form, to their CV being extracted and read, scored by an AI Agent against role criteria, to a result email sent automatically to shortlisted and non-shortlisted candidates alike.

Beyond automation, I'm also comfortable on the visual and content side — design in Canva and website management in WordPress — so a project can be handled end-to-end, from the system to what people actually see.

Saya bekerja di titik temu antara otomasi dan AI — merancang workflow n8n yang menggantikan proses manual berulang: dari form masuk, dokumen diproses, sampai keputusan dibantu AI Agent, tanpa seseorang harus duduk mengerjakannya satu per satu.

Project andalan saya adalah sistem screening CV otomatis — mulai dari pelamar mengisi form, CV-nya diambil dan dibaca, dinilai oleh AI berdasarkan kriteria posisi, sampai email hasil terkirim otomatis ke kandidat lolos maupun tidak.

Selain automation, saya juga terbiasa mengerjakan sisi visual dan konten — desain di Canva dan pengelolaan website di WordPress — jadi satu project bisa saya kerjakan dari sistemnya sampai tampilannya.

// project

CV Screening Automation

A complete workflow from CV submission to candidate response — no manual steps in between.

Alur kerja penuh dari CV masuk sampai kandidat dapat kabar — tanpa campur tangan manual.

n8n · AI Agent · Google Workspace

A recruiting system that scores CVs and replies on its own

Sistem rekrutmen yang menilai CV dan membalasnya sendiri

A candidate fills out an application form, their CV is automatically read and scored by an AI Agent against role criteria (skills, education, experience), then the system sends a different email to shortlisted and non-shortlisted candidates — with results logged automatically to Google Sheets, and error alerts if any step fails.

Kandidat mengisi form lamaran, CV-nya otomatis dibaca dan dinilai oleh AI Agent berdasarkan kriteria posisi (skill, pendidikan, pengalaman), lalu sistem mengirim email berbeda ke kandidat yang lolos maupun tidak — lengkap dengan pencatatan otomatis ke Google Sheets dan notifikasi error kalau ada langkah yang gagal.

01

Form Submission

The applicant submits their details and CV through a form.

Pelamar mengisi data diri dan mengunggah CV lewat form.

02

Extract CV

File is uploaded to Drive, re-downloaded, and its content extracted to text.

File diunggah ke Drive, diunduh ulang, isinya diekstrak jadi teks.

03

CV Scoring

AI Agent reads the role criteria and scores CV fit in a structured format.

AI Agent membaca kriteria posisi dan menilai kecocokan CV dalam format terstruktur.

04

Send Feedback

Send Feedback

Results are logged to a sheet and an email is sent automatically based on status.

Hasil dicatat ke sheet dan email otomatis terkirim sesuai status kelulusan.
